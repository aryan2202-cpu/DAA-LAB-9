Q4: Minimum Cost to Connect Sticks
Input: array L[] of stick lengths, min-heap

Sample 1
  sticks: 2 4 3
  step 1: connect 2 and 3, cost 5
  step 2: connect 4 and 5, cost 9
  minimum total cost = 14

Sample 2
  sticks: 1 8 3 5
  step 1: connect 1 and 3, cost 4
  step 2: connect 4 and 5, cost 9
  step 3: connect 8 and 9, cost 17
  minimum total cost = 30

Sample 3 (equal sticks)
  sticks: 5 5 5 5 5
  step 1: connect 5 and 5, cost 10
  step 2: connect 5 and 5, cost 10
  step 3: connect 5 and 10, cost 15
  step 4: connect 10 and 15, cost 25
  minimum total cost = 60

Validation against exhaustive search over all merge orders: 1000/1000 random tests matched

Time complexity analysis
  Building the min-heap (heapify)       : O(n)
  n-1 rounds, each = 2 extract-min + 1 insert = 3 * O(log n) : O(n log n)
  Total T(n) = O(n) + O(n log n) = O(n log n), space = O(n)
  Correctness: the two shortest sticks are paid for in every later merge of their group, so they should sit deepest in the merge tree (same argument as Huffman)
